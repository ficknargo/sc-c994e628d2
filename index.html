Scrabble Cam

Point your phone at the board. After each turn tap Score turn.

Turn order = this order. Points go to whoever's turn it is, then it rotates.

History

Tap a turn to fix letters, tiles, blanks or who played it. Scores recalculate.

    Settings

    Stored only in this browser (localStorage). Images go straight from your phone to that API. Without a key the app still finds the new tiles; you type their letters.

    End of game & stats

    Enter each other player's leftover tiles as letters (? = blank) or their point total. Each loses their own leftovers; whoever went out gains everyone else's.
